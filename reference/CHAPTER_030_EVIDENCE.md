# Evidence posture

The commandments do not introduce new technical claims.

They compress principles already developed across Chapters 0–29, including:

```text
problem before technology
requirements as verifiable properties
Designed → Codified → Verified → Monitored
functional/domain authority
data ownership
simplicity and fit before fashion
responsibility vs delegated execution
claim-proportional evidence
context before copying another architecture
bounded autonomy / agent governance
```

Their supporting evidence therefore remains inherited from the chapters where those concepts were developed and audited.

Chapter 30 must not be cited as independent proof for a technical recommendation.

# The ten selected principles

```text
I    Non avrai altra architettura all'infuori del problema
II   Non nominerai “requisito” invano
III  Ricordati di santificare l'evidence
IV   Onora il dominio e chi ne possiede la verità
V    Non uccidere la semplicità
VI   Non commettere complessità senza necessità
VII  Non rubare la responsabilità al suo proprietario
VIII Non dare falsa testimonianza sulla qualità del tuo sistema
IX   Non desiderare l'architettura d'altri
X    Non desiderare più autonomia di quanta tu possa governare
```

The wording deliberately mirrors the rhetorical form of the traditional Ten Commandments.

The resemblance is stylistic and mnemonic; the chapter is not used to make religious claims or to reframe the book as a religious analogy.

# Quality floor

The final decalogue must remain:

```text
memorable
supported by the book's substance
tool/vendor independent
compatible with contextual trade-off thinking
serious in technical meaning
lighter/goliardic only in formulation
```

It must not:

```text
become a substitute for the chapters
create context-free architecture rules
introduce new unsupported claims
turn the book into a commandment-driven structure retroactively
```
